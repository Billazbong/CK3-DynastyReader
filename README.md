# Dynasty Chronicle

**Dynasty Chronicle** is a companion tool for *Crusader Kings III* that allows players to explore, analyze, and preserve the history of their campaigns.

By importing a save file, the application extracts campaign data to reconstruct the history of a dynasty, including family trees, characters, titles, successions, and significant events.

## Planned Features

- **Family Tree** — Explore generations, marriages, descendants, and family branches.
- **Historical Timeline** — Browse significant events throughout a campaign.
- **Title History** — Track the evolution of titles and territorial possessions.
- **Character Profiles** — Explore character backgrounds, relationships, and life events.
- **Dynasty Statistics** — Analyze the growth, influence, and legacy of a dynasty.

## Project Goals

Turn *Crusader Kings III* save files into interactive historical archives, allowing players to revisit and explore their campaigns beyond the information provided by the game's interface.
